Research

Active Research.

Three concurrent positions at UBC spanning cognitive neuroscience, neuropathology, and addiction psychiatry.

NC4 Lab — Neural Circuits for Computation, Cognition and ControlApr 2026 – Present

Cognitive Decline Detection via VR Spatial Navigation

Supervisor: Dr. Manu Madhav · UBC Biomedical Engineering

  • Building an ML pipeline on VR spatial navigation data to detect early markers of cognitive decline and Alzheimer's, working with 6DOF sensor streams (head orientation, avatar position, controller quaternions) from 45 participants.
  • XGBoost classifiers with handcrafted movement features (angular velocity, heading drift, rotation smoothness) alongside transformer-based sequence models for time-series classification.
  • Functional PCA and Dynamic Time Warping for feature extraction on variable-length multivariate time series.

Hirsch-Reinshagen Lab — Neuropathology and CNS DisordersMar 2026 – Present

Computational IHC Analysis for CNS Disorders

Supervisor: Dr. Veronica Hirsch-Reinshagen · UBC Pathology / VGH

  • Computational image analysis of multiplexed IHC slides from post-mortem human brain tissue using QuPath for cell segmentation and stain colocalization.
  • ML pipelines for automated spatial profiling of protein markers across brain regions in neurodegeneration and traumatic spinal cord injury.
